What PAE is

Inside-out quantitative analytics

PAE is not a research report about what the world says — it is a measurement instrument for your own book. Import your holdings, and PAE computes the quantities a risk desk would: what you could lose, where the exposure concentrates, and what breaks under stress.

Risk measurement

Value-at-Risk and Conditional VaR, drawdown analysis, correlation structure — quantified on your actual holdings, not a model portfolio.

Factor exposure

Decompose returns into factor loadings so you can see which bets — value, size, momentum, sector, rates — are really driving the book.

Monte Carlo scenarios

Forward-looking outcome distributions from simulated paths: the range of what could happen, with probabilities, not point forecasts.

Stress tests

Historical and hypothetical shocks applied to your positions. See the portfolio's breaking points before the market finds them.

Decision journal

Log the rationale behind each decision alongside the numbers, so hindsight has evidence instead of stories.

Analytics companions

The same analytics as standalone Excel and Google Sheet workbooks for offline or spreadsheet-native workflows — with one explicit boundary: spreadsheets are not encrypted.

Privacy architecture

What is encrypted, and what is not

PAE runs entirely on your own machine — there is no PAE cloud and no account, and both processes bind to loopback. Within that, here is the encryption posture, stated plainly:

  1. Key derivation happens in your browser. Your passphrase is stretched with Argon2id — client-side, in the dashboard app. The passphrase never leaves your device.
  2. Vault records are encrypted before storage. Decision-journal entries and other versioned records are encrypted with AES-256-GCM in your browser; the engine stores ciphertext only and holds no passphrase, no derived key, and no decryption path for them.
  3. The analytics working store is a local file, unencrypted. Holdings you import for analysis sit in a SQLite database in your PAE data directory — readable by anyone who can read your files, like any local app's data file. The knowledge base is the same, unless its notes were ingested with an encryption key through the PKE library; the dashboard labels an unencrypted knowledge base plainly.
  4. Recovery is yours to set up — Shamir 3-of-5. PAE includes Shamir secret sharing for the vault key: split your recovery key into 5 shares, any 3 reconstruct it. Nobody — including us — holds your key or your shares, and there is no recovery service. If you lose your passphrase and fewer than 3 shares survive, the encrypted records are unrecoverable. That is the price of real client-side encryption, stated up front.
What we do not claim. No live brokerage connections (imports are files you provide), no FX conversion engine (holdings are tracked per currency), no encryption at rest for imported holdings (see point 3), and no investment advice — PAE computes and explains; it never recommends. The privacy posture is exactly the four points above.

Standing disclosures

The fine print, up front

  • Educational analytics tool, not a financial advisor. PAE computes risk numbers; it does not give investment advice.
  • The tool calculates; you decide. Every number on this site and in the dashboard is a measurement, never a recommendation.
  • No buy/sell recommendations. PAE outputs analytics, not trade signals. It will never tell you what to buy or sell.
  • We never sell user data. PAE has no cloud and no telemetry: your data lives on your machine, and there is nothing on a server of ours to sell.
  • Spreadsheets are not encrypted. The Excel and Google Sheet companions are separate, standalone artifacts without PAE's encryption guarantees.
