Privacy — PAE

PAE (Personal Analytics Engine) is a product of 2351485 ALBERTA INC. This page describes the self-hosted AGPL edition.

What the server stores

Ciphertext only. Holdings, accounts, portfolios, journal entries and knowledge content reach the server already encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM, key derived from your passphrase via Argon2id). The server holds no passphrase, no derived key, and no decryption path.

What we collect

Nothing. The self-hosted edition has no accounts system, no analytics, no trackers, and no telemetry. Market prices and factor data are fetched from public data sources when you run an analysis; those requests carry no portfolio data.

Recovery

Recovery is by Shamir 3-of-5 secret sharing, set up and held by you. We cannot recover your data for you — that is the design, not an omission.
